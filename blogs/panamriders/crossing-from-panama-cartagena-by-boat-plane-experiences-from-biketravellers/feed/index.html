Just to let you know that Aires Airo doesn’t have any direct flights to Cartagena at the moment (september 2010)
The shortest flight will take you more than 8 hours, you have to stop twice first in Perreira and then in Bogota, in Bogota you have to change planes.

Direct flights are available at Copa Airlines and Avianca, but way more expensive and they also charge 100 dollar a bike
